Meet the hosts

The President & vice-President

Rev. Samson & Stella Ajetomobi

Rev. Samson and Rev. Stella Ajetomobi are dedicated ministers of the Gospel and visionary leaders with a shared passion for advancing God’s Kingdom, developing leaders, and strengthening families. Rev. Samson is the President of The Men of Issachar Vision Incorporated (MIV), founded in 1989, and Overseer of the Redemption Faith Churches. A trained Agricultural Engineer and certified professional manager, he is a leadership developer, author, and seasoned minister with a mandate to reach the unreached and awaken the Church to her divine responsibilities.

Rev. Stella Ajetomobi, Vice President of MIV, is a gifted teacher and prophetic minister with a passion for Christian character, discipleship, and missions. A former Food Technologist at the International Institute of Tropical Agriculture (IITA) with a Master’s degree in Personnel Psychology, she is an author and host of Back to Bethel Retreats, a ministry dedicated to strengthening ministers’ wives and female leaders. Together, they have devoted their lives to ministry, leadership development, and building godly homes, raising a generation committed to fulfilling God’s purpose
